Brand Identity

Becoming Studios

Client Becoming Studios
Services Brand Strategy, Logo, Visual Identity
Year 2024

The Challenge

Becoming Studios was starting from scratch and needed a visual identity that reflected its vision. The client wanted a clean, minimal aesthetic and initially imagined incorporating butterflies to represent transformation. The challenge was finding a way to honor that symbolism while creating something refined, modern, and distinctive.

The Approach

Instead of using literal butterfly imagery, we explored a more subtle interpretation through arching patterns that brought texture and movement into the design. We paired those details with elegant typography, a custom monogram, and a warm, neutral color palette to create an identity that felt sophisticated without being overly complicated.

The Result

A cohesive brand identity that balances simplicity with thoughtful detail. From branded materials to the digital experience, Becoming Studios now has a consistent visual foundation that reflects its vision and can grow alongside the business.

Brand Identity

My Wellness Weigh-In

Client Kristin Jennings
Services Brand Identity, Logo Design, Creative Direction
Year 2023

The Challenge

My Wellness Weigh-In was preparing for a new chapter. With plans to expand beyond weight management into mental, physical, and spiritual wellness, the client needed a brand that reflected her bigger vision. The existing logo focused heavily on fitness and weight loss, but the business was growing into something much more comprehensive.

The Approach

We moved away from traditional fitness imagery and developed a more thoughtful, modern identity. I incorporated a subtle scale needle into the logo as a nod to the brand's name and history, while introducing refined typography and an earthy color palette to communicate warmth, balance, and well-being. The goal was to create something fresh and inviting without losing the familiarity of the original brand.

The Result

A refreshed identity that honors where My Wellness Weigh-In began while making room for where it's headed. The new look brings greater clarity to the brand's vision and gives the client a cohesive foundation for its next chapter.

Event Branding

Project Name

Client Book Trust
Services Creative Direction, Event Branding, Digital & Print Collateral
Year 2026

The Challenge

Books on Tap is Book Trust's annual fundraising event in Colorado. With the organization recently undergoing a rebrand, this year's event presented a unique opportunity to introduce its new look ahead of the official reveal. The challenge was to bring that refreshed identity into the event's marketing while keeping the fun, energetic personality that makes Books on Tap recognizable.

The Approach

I incorporated Book Trust's new colors, typography, and graphic elements into the existing event branding, creating a fresh look that still felt familiar. I carried that direction across promotional emails, social graphics, event signage, presentations, and the donation landing page, creating a consistent experience leading up to the fundraiser.

The Result

A refreshed event campaign that gave Books on Tap a familiar yet updated look while introducing elements of Book Trust's new identity ahead of its official reveal.

Brand Identity & Web Design

Curl IQ

Client Quintina Brunson
Services Creative Direction, Brand Identity, Logo Design, Website Design
Year 2025

The Challenge

As Curl IQ prepared to launch its first hair tool—a brush designed to distribute hair products evenly—the client needed a brand that brought beauty and technology together. The existing identity leaned toward traditional beauty, but the vision was something more innovative, modern, and appealing to women of different ethnicities and hair types.

The Approach

Taking inspiration from the sophistication of tech brands and the personality of modern beauty brands, I developed an identity that brought both worlds together. The new logo features flowing, wave-like elements inspired by the movement and texture of hair, paired with modern typography and muted jewel tones. I carried that direction into a product-focused website designed to introduce the brush, highlight its features, and build excitement for its launch.

The Result

A refreshed brand identity and digital experience that give Curl IQ a distinct presence in the beauty-tech space and a strong foundation for introducing its first product.

Book Design

Beets by Crystal Book

Client Crystal Robinson
Services Book Cover Design, Interior Typesetting
Year 2024

The Challenge

For her first book, Beets by Crystal, author Crystal Robinson needed a design that would bring her vision to life and make a strong first impression. The goal was to create a book that felt fresh, engaging, and reflective of her approach to food, while making the content easy for readers to navigate.

The Approach

I leaned into the natural beauty of the ingredients, using rich food photography, bold green typography, and vibrant color to give the cover an eye-catching presence. Inside, I developed a clean, organized layout that balanced recipes, imagery, and supporting content, making the book both visually appealing and easy to follow.

The Result

A vibrant, professionally designed debut publication that brings Crystal's passion for food to life, with a bold cover that draws readers in and an interior designed to keep them engaged.
